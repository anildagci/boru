What is BÖRÜ?
BÖRÜ is a free, open-source desktop companion for people who build games and projects with AI (Claude Code, Codex). It sits in the corner of your screen as a small wolf.
• Live wolf: it watches Claude Code work; it gets busy while files are edited, warns you about a risky command (e.g. a delete) and cheers when the job is done. Turn it on with right-click > Live wolf.
• Approval: it asks you before its own permanent steps (such as delivery); the answer comes only from your click.
• Working method: rules and checklists so you lose less work with AI.
• 3D pack: from a drawing to a game-ready 3D character with your installed Blender.

What do you need?
• Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
• Recommended: the Claude desktop app (Code tab) or Codex. On first launch BÖRÜ offers to connect to them.
• For the 3D pack only: Blender 5 (blender.org). BÖRÜ finds it on its own.

Where does BÖRÜ write?
• Installation: your own user folder (no administrator rights needed).
• Your settings, log and your own mascots: Documents\BORU
• Claude Code / Codex settings: only if you click "Connect" or "Turn on", after a backup, only BÖRÜ's own entries.
• Nothing leaves your computer by default.

Good to know (honestly)
• For now the live wolf only watches and warns; it cannot stop Claude's actions. Asking before actions and undo come next.
• The live wolf works with Claude Code only; not in the Claude chat or Cowork.
• BÖRÜ is a rule-based tool; it is not an AI model itself.
• The installer is unsigned: Windows may show "Unknown publisher" ("More info" > "Run anyway").
• Uninstalling keeps Documents\BORU and your Claude/Codex settings.
