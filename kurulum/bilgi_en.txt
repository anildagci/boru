What is BÖRÜ?
BÖRÜ is a helper that drives your installed Blender along the path from a drawing to a game-ready 3D character. Its real strength is connecting to AI models (Claude Code, Codex): the model does the work, BÖRÜ measures every step, explains it in writing, stops anything that breaks the rules and asks you before permanent actions (such as delivery).

What do you need?
• Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
• Blender 5 (blender.org). BÖRÜ finds it on its own.
• Recommended: the Claude desktop app (Code tab) or Codex. On first launch BÖRÜ offers to connect to them.

Where does BÖRÜ write?
• Installation: your own user folder (no administrator rights needed).
• Your settings, log and your own mascots: Documents\BORU
• Claude Code / Codex settings: only if you click "Connect", after a backup, only the "boru" entry.
• Your character folders: it never touches source files and never deletes an attempt.

Good to know (honestly)
• BÖRÜ is a rule-based tool; it is not an AI model itself.
• The head-to-body step is tested on two collar types so far (t-shirt neckline, high jacket collar); others may need tuning.
• The installer is unsigned: Windows may show "Unknown publisher" ("More info" > "Run anyway").
• Uninstalling keeps Documents\BORU and your Claude/Codex settings.
